Track your order

Your YOGAMATY™ is made to order. Here is what happens after you pre-purchase, and the emails you will receive along the way.

  1. Order receivedYour pre-purchase is confirmed by email. Production capacity is organized in batches of approximately 50 pieces.
  2. In productionGlobal Women’s Handicrafts coordinates materials and an independent woman artisan weaves your piece, which is then collected, inspected, finished and quality-checked in Nepal.
  3. Batch shippedThe completed batch ships from Nepal. Under the working plan it travels in bulk to New Haven, Connecticut.
  4. Shipped to youYour individual order ships from New Haven, with tracking details by email.

Typical timing. Our target is delivery within 60 days of your pre-purchase. It is a target, not a guarantee — the first order in a batch waits longest. If delivery will miss the target, we will email you a revised date and offer cancellation with a full refund. {{ ph('ADD TYPICAL DURATION OF EACH STAGE ONCE CONFIRMED') }}
