    Where the two materials meet the customer

    Consumer purchase

    The consumer pre-purchases YOGAMATY through yogamaty.com. That purchase helps provide the working capital needed to start production, and connects one consumer with one individual artisan production opportunity.

    It is a purchase, not a donation and not a loan. The way it is organized is meant to create a more direct, transparent and equitable relationship between the consumer and the people whose labor produces the piece.

    The target is delivery within 60 days of the original pre-purchase. That is a proposed target, not yet a guaranteed commitment. See Shipping and Delivery and How It Works.
