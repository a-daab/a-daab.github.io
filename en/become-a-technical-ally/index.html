The program

An invitation to help build the technical foundation.

Empowerment Collective will invite technical contributors to participate in the development of TransparaTrade™ through its Allies Program. The program seeks contributions from software engineers, programming-language designers, cybersecurity specialists, open-source developers, data architects, researchers, UX designers, technical writers, and others with relevant expertise.

The objective is to bring professional knowledge into a survivor-led initiative. The Allies Program is not presented as a general volunteer opportunity. It is an invitation to help build the technical foundation for TransparaTrade.

Survivor leadership and the value of knowledge

Empowerment Collective is a survivor-led organization. The Allies Program is intended to recognize the value of different forms of knowledge: lived experience, indigenous knowledge, traditional craftsmanship, academic research, and professional expertise. The absence of academic accreditation does not mean the absence of expertise.

Survivor leadership is not about replacing academic knowledge with lived experience. It is about leveling the playing field so that the wisdom gained through surviving injustice holds equal authority in shaping the solutions intended to end it. Survivor leaders guide the purpose, values, and direction of the project. Technical Allies contribute their specialized knowledge to help make that vision possible.
