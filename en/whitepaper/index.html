Whitepaper

The TransparaTrade white paper is currently being amended. The updated version is intended to address the more detailed technical and operational aspects of the project. These include agricultural sourcing, hemp farming, material processing, electricity usage, production documentation, supply-chain verification, privacy-preserving identification, offline recording, and open-source technical architecture.

The current target is to complete the amendments by the end of 2026. An explanatory video is also planned. The white paper will help establish the technical foundation for the next stage of TransparaTrade development. It is not described as completed until the amendments have been finalized and approved.
