Who is responsible for your data

Empowerment Collective, a nonprofit organization based in New Haven, Connecticut, operates this website and is the controller of the personal data described here. To ask a question or make a request about your data, write to [ADD DATA-REQUEST EMAIL] or [ADD POSTAL ADDRESS].

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Stripe's hosted checkout sets its own on Stripe's domain.

What we collect, why, and for how long

Signing the petition, applying as a Technical Ally and sending a wholesale inquiry each collect only what the form asks for. Nothing is collected for advertising and we run no analytics that set cookies.

Buying a YOGAMATY™

Checkout is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe collects your name, email address, shipping address and payment details, and shares your order, name, email and shipping address with us so we can ship your order and respond to you. Stripe is an independent processor and has its own privacy policy. We keep order records as long as needed for fulfillment, returns, accounting and tax obligations.

Basis: performing the sale (contract); legal obligations.

Signing the petition

We store your name, email address, location, the time you signed, and that you confirmed the petition's demand. The petition list is kept in a database on Cloudflare's infrastructure. We use your email address only to prevent duplicate signatures and to count signatures. Signing does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Signatures are retained for the foreseeable future; Empowerment Collective leadership decides whether and when they are deleted. The list may be presented publicly or to legislators. You can ask us to remove your signature at any time (see below).

Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw.

Applying to become a Founding Technical Ally

The application form sends what you enter (full name, email, optional company, organization or professional role, primary area of expertise, your statement, and your acknowledgment that this is an initial voluntary expression of interest) by email to the Empowerment Collective team. The website itself does not store your application. A copy remains in the team's email inbox, where it is retained for the foreseeable future; Empowerment Collective leadership decides on deletion. A résumé or portfolio is not required at the initial stage; we may request more information from selected applicants later.

Basis: your consent; our interest in considering applicants.

Wholesale and studio inquiries

The inquiry form emails your name, phone number, email, business name and note to our team. The website does not store it. A copy remains in the team's email inbox.

Basis: your consent; steps you ask us to take before entering a contract.

Technical data

To protect the forms from abuse we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which runs a short check in your browser without cookies or tracking for advertising, and we briefly use your IP address to limit how fast a single device can submit a form. IP addresses are not kept in our petition database. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may keep its own server logs, including IP addresses.
