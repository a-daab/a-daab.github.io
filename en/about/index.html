The organization

A 100% survivor-led nonprofit.

Empowerment Collective is a 100% survivor-led nonprofit dedicated to ending modern slavery, based in New Haven, Connecticut, and working in Nepal. It trains and employs the women artisans who make every YogaMaty.

The organization works from the understanding that systemic vulnerability, gendered expectation, and increasing climate risk put women in Nepal at risk of extreme poverty, forced marriage, forced labor and other forms of exploitation. The transparency and human-rights protections woven into the YogaMaty supply chain mean that making this product is itself an opportunity for economic empowerment, through a living income, for every woman involved.
