Your yoga mat

YOGAMATY™ The conscious layer between you and your yoga mat

YOGAMATY™ is not simply another yoga product. It is a traditionally handwoven yoga mat cover designed to connect conscious consumers directly with the women artisans whose traditional knowledge, labor, and craftsmanship create the product.

The product

Handwoven from natural hemp and reclaimed sari textiles.

YOGAMATY weaves together traditional Nepali arts knowledge, agricultural materials, textile recovery, and contemporary consumer demand to create the first transparent yoga mat cover.

Place YOGAMATY directly on top of your yoga mat for a washable textile layer between your body and the conventional mat underneath. The conventional mat continues to provide cushioning and support. YOGAMATY enables you to maintain both physical hygiene and spiritual alignment.

How it’s made

The consumer is the start of the process, not the end.

Each YOGAMATY is crafted through a Conscious Consumer-Funded Production model. Instead of producing large quantities of products in advance and then attempting to sell them, the consumer begins the process by pre-purchasing a YOGAMATY. That purchase helps provide the working capital needed to initiate production.

This model creates a more direct, transparent, and equitable relationship between the consumer and the people whose labor, knowledge, skills, and cultural heritage are embedded in their product. This is an invitation to engage in cultural participation, not cultural appropriation.

The supply chain

Document the steps. Protect the people.

Over 27 million people are trapped in forced labor worldwide. Their stories remain invisible, in products we purchase every day, because of opaque supply chains. YOGAMATY is the first TransparaTrade pilot product—the beginning of the end of hidden suffering.

From the two distinct material journeys—hemp and reclaimed sari fabric—to processing, weaving, shipping, and distribution, every step of the YOGAMATY supply chain is traced and visible to consumers.
