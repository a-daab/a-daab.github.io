The details

What it is.

What it isA yoga mat towel/cover, placed directly on top of an existing conventional yoga mat. Not intended to be used as a standalone yoga mat.
Dimensions68 in (L) × 24 in (W) × 3 mm thick · 173 × 61 cm
Finished weight[ADD — remains to be confirmed; published once measured]
CompositionNatural fiber with reclaimed materials. Face: hemp warp with weft strips cut from reclaimed post-consumer sari fabric. Backing: handwoven hemp cloth, no artificial dyes. Bound edge with knotted fringe at both short ends.
Primary textile inputsNatural hemp yarn about 650 g; reclaimed sari fabric about 484 g; combined about 1,134 g — about 61.5% hemp and 38.5% reclaimed sari by weight. These are the percentages of the two principal textile inputs, not of the finished product. Reclaimed saris may contain mixed fibers.
CareCan be washed on a gentle machine cycle and air-dried. Hand-washing is recommended for longer-lasting care.
DeliveryMade to order; target delivery within 60 days of the pre-purchase.
MadeBy independent women artisans in Nepal, on traditional handlooms, coordinated through Global Women’s Handicrafts.

YOGAMATY is a premium, traditionally handwoven yoga mat towel/cover produced using natural hemp yarn and reclaimed sari textiles. The product brings together traditional weaving knowledge, agricultural materials, textile recovery, and contemporary consumer demand.

Because the weft comes from reclaimed saris, no two pieces are alike: the stripes, colors and rhythm of the weave differ from piece to piece. That is the imperfection that proves it was made by hand.

Product development. The current product is in its pilot and prototype stage. Approximately 20 artisans have contributed to the initial development. The estimated production capacity in Kathmandu is approximately 50 products per batch, subject to further operational confirmation. Additional testing should examine grip, movement, bunching, durability, washing, and performance when used on top of conventional yoga mats; we make no promises about those until it is complete. Final finished-product weight, other components, and packaging measurements remain to be confirmed.

It is the initial pilot of TransparaTrade™. Follow the supply chain — and when records are published, scan the label to open the record for your piece in the Supply-Chain Explorer.
