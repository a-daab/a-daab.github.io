The larger movement

Make supply-chain transparency a basic expectation of global trade.

The larger movement behind this project is End Modern Slavery Now! The movement seeks to challenge the systems that allow human exploitation to remain hidden within global commerce. A central objective is to advocate mandatory supply-chain transparency labeling.

Consumers are often provided information about what a product contains and how to care for it. Yet they may know very little about the people whose labor created the product. The movement seeks to make supply-chain transparency a basic expectation of global trade.

The objective

Not voluntary claims. Accountability.

The objective is not simply to encourage companies to make voluntary ethical claims.

It is to advocate systems in which meaningful supply-chain information must be documented, communicated, and subject to accountability.

Mandatory labeling would require appropriate public policy, verification standards, enforcement, and worker protections.

Conscious consumption and conscious capitalism

Conscious consumption determines where the money goes.

YOGAMATY™ seeks to connect conscious consumption with a more transparent understanding of how purchasing power is used. The consumer’s purchase is intended to help initiate a production opportunity. The product is made through traditional craftsmanship.

Conscious capitalism determines how the money is used.

TransparaTrade seeks to make the value of each contribution and the fairness of the exchange visible. The supply-chain system seeks to document the events and economic exchanges involved.

Not simply conscious consumption. Conscious participation.

The larger purpose is to demonstrate that purchasing can become a more conscious form of participation in the relationships behind global trade.
